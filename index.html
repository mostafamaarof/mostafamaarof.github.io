About

A career built on the ledger — and now on the models behind it.

Supreme Audit Institution leader with 19+ years spanning external audit of United Nations and international organizations, IT and AI audit, cybersecurity, and ISSAI-based performance audit. I currently serve on the external audit team of the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), and previously audited UNIDO and the African Union Commission — co-authoring INTOSAI's guidance on auditing Artificial Intelligence solutions and cloud computing along the way. As a certified INTOSAI Development Initiative (IDI) trainer, I design and deliver capacity-building programs for Supreme Audit Institutions across Africa and the Arab region, and speak regularly at INTOSAI, EUROSAI and ARABOSAI forums on AI audit, big data and digital transformation.
